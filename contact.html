Quick answers

Frequently asked

What's your return policy?

30 days, no questions asked, on anything unopened. If your pet refuses a food or treat, bring it back even if it's open — we'll refund or swap it.

How fast is delivery?

Same-day within Riverton for orders placed before 2pm. 1–3 business days nationwide. Club members on the Adventurer plan or higher get free delivery with no minimum.

Do you price-match?

Yes, against any authorized retailer. Show us the listing and we'll match it at the register.

Can I bring my pet into the store?

Always — leashed or carried, all species welcome. We keep water bowls by the door and treats at the register.

How do I book a grooming appointment?

Call the store or use the form above. Club members get priority booking and 10% off every service.
