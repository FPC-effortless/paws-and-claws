Quick answers

Frequently asked

What counts as a pet emergency?

Difficulty breathing, prolonged vomiting or diarrhea, suspected poisoning, trauma or bleeding, inability to urinate, and sudden lethargy in a pet that is usually active. When in doubt, call the hotline — a licensed vet will triage over the phone and tell you whether to come in.

How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Open your member portal, head to Bookings, and reschedule or cancel free of charge up to 24 hours before your appointment. Inside 24 hours, call the store and we'll do our best to fill the slot.

Which vaccines does my pet need for grooming or boarding?

Dogs need Rabies, DHPP and Bordetella; cats need Rabies and FVRCP. Upload records in My pets and our staff verifies them within one business day — you only upload once.

Can I return food if my pet won't eat it?

Yes. Bring back up to half the bag or cans within 30 days with your receipt for a full refund or exchange. Opened food is donated to the Riverton shelter, so nothing goes to waste.

Do you offer payment plans for surgery?

Spay/neuter and dental procedures can be split into three equal payments at checkout. Members on Adult Adventurer or Senior Snuggler plans pay no interest.

How does the adoption marketplace work?

Every listing is health-checked and vaccinated before it goes live. Send an inquiry from the listing page and the breeder or shelter replies within one business day. A deposit holds your new pet once you've met.

Prefer to do it yourself?

Your member portal has bookings, orders, pets, messages and payment methods — all editable, any time of day.
