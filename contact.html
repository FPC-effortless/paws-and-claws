Quick answers

Frequently asked

What counts as a pet emergency?

Difficulty breathing, prolonged vomiting or diarrhea, suspected poisoning, trauma or bleeding, inability to urinate, and sudden lethargy in a pet that is usually active. When in doubt, call the hotline — a licensed vet will triage over the phone and tell you whether to come in.

How do I cancel or reschedule an appointment?

Manage upcoming appointments from Bookings in your member portal. Contact the store directly if you need help changing an appointment.

Which vaccines does my pet need for grooming or boarding?

Dogs need Rabies, DHPP and Bordetella; cats need Rabies and FVRCP. Upload records in My pets for staff review before booking a service that requires current vaccines.

Can I return food if my pet won't eat it?

Ask the store team about returns and exchanges. Keep your receipt and contact us before bringing an item back.

Do you offer payment plans for surgery?

Payment plans are not available through this site yet. Contact the store team to discuss payment options before booking.

How does the adoption marketplace work?

Review the health information shown on each listing and contact the listed organization with questions. This site records inquiries; response times and adoption arrangements depend on the listing provider.

Prefer to do it yourself?

Your member portal lets you manage your pets, appointments and messages. Online ordering and saved payment methods are not available yet.
