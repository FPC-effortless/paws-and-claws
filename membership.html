Membership FAQ

Is the Puppy Pass really free?

Yes. No card required, no trial period, no auto-upgrade. You get the core perks forever, and you'll only ever be asked to upgrade if you want the deeper discounts.

Can I cancel anytime?

Anytime, from your account page. Cancellation is immediate and there's no fee. You keep your perks until the end of the period you've paid for.

Do member discounts stack with sales?

Yes — your member discount applies on top of already-reduced items, both online and in store.

How do birthday surprises work?

Add your pet's gotcha day to your profile. We mail a free treat box and a card the week of. Multi-pet households get one box per pet.

Is my payment information stored?

This is a demo site, so no payment is ever processed and no card is ever collected. On the real store, payments are handled by our PCI-compliant processor — we never see your card number.
